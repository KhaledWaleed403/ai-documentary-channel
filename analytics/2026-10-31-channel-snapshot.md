## What this does and doesn't show

**Measured:**
- On Instagram, 4 of 12 Reels moved past an initial pool of roughly 100–140 views to 1.4–1.6K. Those four had the lowest skip rates (32–38%, against 43–64% for the rest) and the longest average watch times (15–20 s, against 6–12 s).
- SubmarineMessages had the best hold on both Instagram (20.3 s average) and TikTok (29.0 s average on a 46 s video; every other TikTok sits at 4–9.5 s).
- TikTok average watch time is 4–9.5 s on 26–49 s videos. Most viewers leave in the first quarter.
- YouTube: SuperheatedWater is the only video with meaningful views (188; 49.8% APV).

**Hypotheses (not proven, small samples):**
1. Openers built as a **contradiction** held better: "Water can get hotter than boiling — and still not boil", "Seawater soaks up radio — so how does a hidden submarine get a message?", "cleans its own air… so why does it ever come home?", "It's done — but your food isn't." Openers that **state a fact** did worse ("A nuclear submarine makes its own oxygen by splitting water" had a 63.8% skip rate). But the plate Short also opens on a contradiction and did not break out, so this is a lean, not a rule.
2. Posting a whole batch within a few minutes may split early distribution: in each batch only two Reels left the initial pool. Spacing posts out is a cheap test.
3. TikTok viewers leave in the first 5–8 s. The first two seconds need on-screen motion and the contradiction stated, not set-up.
