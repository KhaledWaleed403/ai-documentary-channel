## Deliberately not claimed
- How often superheating eruptions happen (no source gives a frequency). The Short says nothing about "rare".
- Any specific temperature target, penetration depth or standing time beyond "at least three minutes".
- That a cool water-cup result makes a dish safe, or that all of any one material is safe or unsafe.
- What exactly the candy bar was, any words Spencer said or thought, or anyone else present.
