# Episode 001 — Research Notes

## Core question
How does a microwave oven heat food when the air inside stays at about room temperature?

## Mechanism in one paragraph
A **magnetron** turns electrical power into microwaves at ~2.45 GHz (C02, C13). Inside the magnetron, a hot cathode releases electrons, a magnet curves their paths, and the electrons make cavities in the anode resonate. A waveguide sends the output into the cooking cavity (C15). The **metal walls reflect** the waves (C04), and the overlapping reflections form a **standing-wave pattern** of stronger and weaker regions (C10). The waves' **electric field alternates** billions of times a second (C05). **Polar molecules**, mainly water but also fats and sugars (C16), are pushed and twisted by it (C06). In doing so the field **transfers energy to the food**. Collisions **randomize** that driven motion, and disordered molecular motion *is* thermal energy (C07). This is **dielectric heating, not resonance** (C08). In thicker food the waves reach only part of the way in; the centre warms as heat **conducts** inward (C09). Heating is uneven, so a **turntable** keeps food moving. That helps, but stirring is still recommended (C11).

## Visual metaphors (state once, keep consistent)
- **Teal** = electromagnetic wave / electric field (arrows for field direction).
- **Orange → gold** = absorbed energy / heat (orange = heating, gold = hot).
- **Mist blue** = cold food / cool air.

## Common misconceptions (and our counter)
1. *Resonance with water* → never use the word "resonate" in narration or on screen.
2. *Inside-out cooking*, and its opposite *surface-only heating* → waves fade into a thick outer zone, then heat spreads to the centre (SH03, SH05).
3. *Turntable makes it even* → "Still — give it a stir" (SH06).
4. *Radioactive food* → payoff line (C12).
5. *Literal molecules* → schematic badge on SH04 and SH05's close-up; heat shown as colour, not particles.
