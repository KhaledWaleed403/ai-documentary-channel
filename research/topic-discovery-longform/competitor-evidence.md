# Competitor Evidence — Verified Video Examples

All numbers come from public YouTube pages, read on **2026-10-29**. "Exact date" comes from a watch page's publish date; otherwise the age is YouTube's relative label. **Baseline** is the view range of the channel's other recent uploads on its `/videos` tab. The **outlier multiple** is an **estimate** (video views ÷ typical recent upload) and is interpretation, not a YouTube metric. Velocity is not available publicly; "≈/day" figures are views ÷ age, i.e. estimates.

LF = long-form, S = Short.

**Context, outside the 90-day window or a different format:**
- [How Submarine Sailors Sleep, Eat, and Survive for Months Underwater](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JgjesjhZlgs): IMD Aviation (98.2K subs), 2026-01-20, 16:21, 3,983,629 views. Shows lasting demand for the crew-survival angle and is the **closest direct competitor** to the recommended framing.
- [24 Hours Onboard a Nuclear Submarine in the Arctic](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FKg3f1c4hyU): Sam Eckholm, ~1 month, 31:07, 7.7M views. Live action, so not an animation competitor, but a strong recent demand signal.
- [Living Underwater: How Submarines Work](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gdy0gBVWAzE): Wendover Productions, 7 years, 11.0M. A classic that still ranks.
- Second Order Science, [How Submarines Became Impossible to Find](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_bdmnZyp8xU): 155K, 7 months.

**Counter-evidence (saturation):**
- The search "how submarines stay underwater for months" returns 20+ Shorts from the last year, almost all at **0.2K–7K views**, plus small long-forms at 11–4,293 views (e.g. Submarine Insider 4.3K; HIDDEN TITANS 479; The Logic Behind 11). The generic "stays underwater for months / makes oxygen" framing is **crowded with low-effort, often AI-generated videos that underperform**.
- What wins is a **specific impossible-sounding problem** ("navigate without GPS", "hear subs 3,000 miles away", "survive 1,000 PSI") or a **named, extreme vessel** (Typhoon).
- Exploded Works already covers dive/surface, sonar, air and water, the reactor and weapons in one 10-minute cutaway.

**Repeatability:** three unrelated channels show a clear submarine breakout in roughly the last 90 days: Second Order Science, SYG DESIGNWORKS and Exploded Works. History of Simple Things is a **probable** fourth; its 136K is high, but its baseline could not be checked because the uploads visible on its channel tab were all under 3 days old.

Within Second Order Science, its three submarine videos are its three best uploads **of the last 5 months**. Over 6 months that no longer holds: "How Radar Jamming Actually Works" has 411K. This is still the clearest "topic, not fluke" signal in this research.

**Baseline caveats:**
- Exploded Works' low-view uploads (651–6.3K) are only 1–13 days old, so its outlier multiple is rough. Its plane (259K) and car-engine (48K) videos show it can also break out on non-submarine topics.
- SYG uploads rarely; there was a 5-month gap before the Typhoon video.

**Recent momentum:** 3 of the long-forms are ≤60 days old; 2 Shorts at 100K+ are ≤3 days old (but their creators' baselines are unknown).

**Format:** both long-form and Shorts perform. The strongest long-forms are 10–23 minutes.
