## Justification per candidate

**1. Submarine life support (8.40).**
- *Strongest evidence:* Second Order Science (26.7K subs) at 1.2M, 406K and 113K on three submarine videos, against a 6K–29K baseline. Exploded Works (2.19K subs) at 156K. SYG (93.4K subs) at 2.14M. History of Simple Things at 136K. All four within roughly the last 90 days.
- *Uncertainty:* generic "how subs stay underwater for months" Shorts and small long-forms are flooded and mostly get under 7K. IMD Aviation's 4.0M crew-survival video (9 months old) and Exploded Works' cutaway overlap the subject. Originality depends on the framing (see recommended-topic.md).
- *Feasibility 6:* one reusable cutaway asset, but many subsystems to draw.

**2. Anaesthesia (7.35).**
- *Strongest evidence:* Ty Rogers MD (2.47K subs) at ~536K and 273K; Docua (2.93K subs) at 66K in 11 days; a Short at 850K (43 days); This. at 1.8M.
- *Uncertainty:* This. and TED-Ed (19.7M) already own the "nobody knows how it works" hook. The small-channel wins rely on clinician authority. The mechanism is partly unresolved science, so a lot of the runtime would go to caveats. Medical sensitivity is higher (fear of waking up, risk claims).

**3. Inside an AI chip (6.80).**
- *Strongest evidence:* Silicon Up Close (9.56K subs) has four videos between 147K and 919K in under a month. Veritasium fiber optics shows the appetite for hardware explainers.
- *Uncertainty:* almost all the small-channel signal comes from one creator. Generic CPU/GPU explainers are saturated at under 1K views. The subject moves fast, so accuracy risk is high.

**4. Digestion (6.60).**
- *Strongest evidence:* two independent channels (764K and 332K) within a month.
- *Uncertainty:* the anatomy-catalogue format is crowded; the visual style overlaps heavily with medical 3D channels; the arc is linear with no single mystery.

**5. Undersea cables (6.60).**
- *Strongest evidence:* 13.9M (Veritasium) and 5.1M (Business Insider) in 2 weeks.
- *Uncertainty:* the evidence comes from very large brands, and current demand is inflated by a geopolitical news story that may fade or bring political baggage.

**6. Dreams (6.60).**
- *Strongest evidence:* 4.0M, 2.1M and 569K recent videos.
- *Uncertainty:* the winners are essay or talk formats; the mechanism is speculative; there is little to animate as a physical system.

**7. Painkillers (6.25).**
- *Strongest evidence:* 7.8M in 24 days.
- *Uncertainty:* a single video; at 2:08 it behaves like a Short; the topic stretches thin at 8 minutes.

**8. Household water pressure (5.90).**
- *Strongest evidence:* Practical Engineering 1.9M.
- *Uncertainty:* one large channel performing at its normal level, so this is not an outlier.

**9. Tap-to-pay (5.85).**
- *Strongest evidence:* Branch Education 738K.
- *Uncertainty:* big brand only; History of Simple Things at 48K is near its norm. Easy to produce.

**10. Plane (5.45).**
- *Strongest evidence:* Exploded Works at 259K on 2.2K subs.
- *Uncertainty:* lift is one of the most-explained topics on YouTube.
