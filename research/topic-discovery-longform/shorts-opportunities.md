**Revised 2026-10-29 after the claim-verification pass.** Claim IDs refer to [claims.md](claims.md).

All Shorts come from the submarine long-form and the same claims file. Rules for each:

- It must make sense with no long-form context: it opens on its own question, never on "as we saw".
- It uses the master sub asset re-framed vertically, labelled "nuclear-powered" where relevant.
- It has burned-in captions and a `.srt` file (per channel rule).
- It ends on its own payoff, with an optional "full story" end card.

**Note on saturation.** Generic titles like "How submarines stay underwater for months" and "How subs make oxygen" are flooded with sub-7K Shorts. Lead with a surprising but **verified** angle.

## Why these three first

1. **The CO₂ Short** still carries the strongest counter-intuitive idea, now as a verified one: air fails in two independent ways, and oxygen alone doesn't fix it. It also has the lowest overlap with the crowded "makes oxygen" Shorts.
2. **The no-GPS Short** mirrors the strongest proven long-form angle in the evidence (1.2M views on a 26.7K-sub channel), and every claim it needs is verified or qualified with good sources.
3. **The message Short** has a surprising answer and a clean wave visual. With the "100 m" figure removed, its claims are qualified but defensible, and little Shorts competition was found for it.
