## Concept
- **Central idea:** What happens after the timer ends: heat keeps moving from the hot outside to the cooler middle with no microwaves involved, so food keeps cooking during standing time.
- **Curiosity gap:** The microwave says it's done. Why isn't the food?
- **Opening hook (new, written for this Short):** "The microwave beeps. It's done — but your food isn't."
- **Arc:** Hook (the beep: done, but the food isn't) → setup (thick food: hot outside, middle catching up) → development (heat keeps flowing inward; microwaves off) → reveal (that's standing time; temperature can climb several more degrees; not extra heating but the same heat spreading) → practical (stand at least 3 minutes, then check with a food thermometer) → payoff (the beep means the waves are done; standing time lets the heat finish the job).
- **Why it stands alone:** Opens on the universal beep moment; the cross-section is introduced on screen.
- **How it differs:** It doesn't cover hot spots, turntables, stirring or molecules (the completed Short's territory). The only physics is conduction after the oven stops, the one idea the other Shorts never cover.
- **Accuracy guardrails:** Standing time is explicitly separated from extra microwave heating (X09). The advice is limited to what S06 and P05 support: at least 3 minutes, then a thermometer (X10). No target temperatures are given.

## Full narration

> The microwave beeps. It's done — but your food isn't.
>
> In thick food, the waves mostly heat the outer layers. When they stop, the outside is hot — the middle is still catching up.
>
> That heat keeps flowing inward, from the hot outside to the cooler center — no microwaves needed.
>
> That's standing time. Food keeps cooking after the oven stops — its temperature can climb several more degrees.
>
> It's not extra heating. It's the same heat, spreading out.
>
> Food-safety guides say let it stand at least three minutes — then check it with a food thermometer.
>
> The beep means the waves are done. Standing time lets the heat finish the job.
