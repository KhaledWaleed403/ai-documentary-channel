## Principles
- The voiceover is the foreground. Music and effects sit well under it and never compete in the 1–4 kHz speech range.
- Use few effects, each tied to a visible action. Nothing on the SH04 field flips, the SH03 ray draws or the text reveals.
- A tonal arc follows the science: **electricity** (dry, low hum) → **microwaves** (airy, high shimmer) → **heat** (warm, low pad).
- The opening hook starts at frame 0. The ending is a musical resolution that lands inside the 41 s, with no added outro.
- No vocals, hard impacts or cartoon sounds.

## Mix targets
- **Integrated loudness:** about **−14 LUFS** for the whole mix (YouTube's playback reference). **True peak ≤ −1 dBTP**, with no clipping.
- **Music under voice:** about **18–20 dB below the VO**. It rises about 6 dB in the gaps: before the first word, between sentences if the read leaves room, and over the ending after the last line.
- **Ducking:** the music volume follows a frame-based curve built from the **narration windows**. These are estimated from the storyboard for now and get replaced by the real VO timings once recorded. Ramps are about 6 frames, so there's no pumping.
- **Effects:** peaks 10–15 dB below the VO. The hum beds (A5, A11) stay barely audible.
- **Ending:** music aligned so the track's own resolution lands at ~40.0 s, with a ≤ 0.5 s fade to silence by frame 1230.
- **Check:** after rendering, run `npx remotion ffmpeg -i <render> -af loudnorm=print_format=json -f null -`. That filter ships with Remotion, so nothing needs installing. It reports integrated LUFS and true peak, and I'll adjust levels to hit the targets.

## Planned code changes (after assets are in place)
- **`src/data/episode001Audio.ts`:** typed cue list. Each cue has its shot ID and frame offset, file path, volume, fades, and loop/trim settings.
- **`src/components/AudioCue.tsx`:** a small reusable wrapper, a `<Sequence>` plus Remotion `<Audio>` with frame-based fade in/out through the `volume` callback. Remotion's built-in `<Audio>` supports `volume`, `loop`, `trimBefore` and `trimAfter`, so no packages are needed.
- **`src/compositions/Episode001.tsx`:**
  - Renders the cue list and the music with a ducking curve built from narration windows.
  - Throws a clear error if a listed audio file is missing, instead of silently rendering without it.
  - Adds a `soundDesign` prop, so the silent draft stays available.
- **`docs/component-inventory.md`:** adds `AudioCue`.
- Scenes, narration, storyboard and visuals stay unchanged.
