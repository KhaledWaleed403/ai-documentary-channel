**Description**

A spacecraft can fly past a planet, fire no engines, and leave faster than it arrived. Seen from the planet, it leaves just as fast as it came in: only its direction changes. Seen from the Sun, the planet's own motion carries that curve along, and the spacecraft leaves faster. The energy is borrowed from the planet's orbit, by an almost imperceptible amount.
