Close the hatch on 145 people and dive — from that moment, every breath they take changes the air they have left. So what keeps a submarine crew alive for weeks, even months, without coming up?

This video goes inside a nuclear-powered submarine to show the hidden machines that solve one problem after another: holding back the sea, scrubbing carbon dioxide out of the air, making oxygen from water, cleaning out every other gas, finding the way without GPS, staying hard to hear, receiving messages through seawater, and powering all of it.

Questions this video answers:
• How does a submarine make oxygen underwater?
• Where does the crew's carbon dioxide go?
• How does a submarine know where it is with no GPS?
• How do you send a message to a submarine that's hiding?
• If the air and power can last, what actually brings a nuclear submarine home?
